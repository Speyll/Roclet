Roclet turns your Android phone into a wireless microphone for a Roc Toolkit receiver.

It captures the phone's microphone and streams it over your local Wi-Fi network using Roc's reliable, low-latency audio transport. A machine on the same network running a ROC receiver - for example the PipeWire or PulseAudio ROC module, or the roc-recv command-line tool - receives it as a normal audio source.

Features:
* Stream the Android microphone to a ROC receiver over Wi-Fi.
* Forward erasure correction (RS8M) with a separate repair stream for resilience on lossy Wi-Fi.
* Configurable destination IP address and source/repair ports, remembered between launches.
* Runs as a foreground service, so streaming continues with the screen off.
* Start and stop from the app or from the ongoing notification.
* Light and dark themes.

Roclet sends audio using Roc's standard rtp+rs8m (source) and rs8m (repair) profiles. The receiver must be reachable on the same network and listening on the configured ports (default source 10001, repair 10002).

Audio is transmitted only to the destination you configure. The app contains no analytics, advertising, or telemetry, and stores only your destination IP and port settings locally on the device.
